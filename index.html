End angles

Lengths accept 3 1/2, 3-1/2, 3.5 or 7/16, measured at the long point. The angle is how far the cut leans from square: two pieces cut at 22.5° meet at 135°, the corner of an octagon. The sliver an angle removes counts as scrap.
