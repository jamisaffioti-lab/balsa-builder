The student kit is one student's share: each sheet cut into 12" thirds, as planned for your 50 students.

Cutting station

Square cuts to length and width. For angled ends, take the pieces to the miter box next.

Lengths accept 3 1/2, 3-1/2, 3.5 or 7/16. Leftover strips and ends go back to stock as offcuts. With angled ends, this length becomes the long point.

Miter box

1 · Set the piece in the box
2 · Swing the blade
Cuts on this piece
    3 · Repeat for matching pieces

    Each cut keeps the long point at the piece's length and trims the rest. Cut an end lying flat and again on edge for a compound cut. Two pieces cut at 22.5° meet at 135°, an octagon corner. Undo at the top reverses a cut.
